Every change of the picture repaints the key. On the D200X a repaint of the wide screen briefly shows the firmware's gauges, so the wide layout draws nothing that ticks (no elapsed time or volume) and uses a coarse bar; pick off for no flashes at all.
Wide composes the image at the D200X screen's 458×196. Stock OpenDeck squeezes it into a square, so the Ulanzi D200X plugin's "Wide screen" action must be set to Action icon with fit Stretch on this layout to expand it again; an OpenDeck that renders the wide key at 458×196 draws it as it is. Press = play / pause.
